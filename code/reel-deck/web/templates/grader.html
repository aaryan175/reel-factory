Keys (laptop) — grade without touching the mouse

A video is one file from the library. It is cut into sections of about 5 seconds (equal cuts, so a 37 s video is 7 sections of 5.3 s). A grade is for one section. The bar under the video has one cell per section — grey ungraded, green HERO, blue B-ROLL, red NEVER — with the white line as the playhead; click a cell to jump there.

1 2 3HERO / B-ROLL / NEVER for the section playing, then straight to the next section. Hold the key to blast through sections at ~6 a second.
Space or Dnext section, no grade (leave it grey). A previous section.
7 8 9HERO / B-ROLL / NEVER for this section and every section after it, then the next video.
S or →next video that still needs grading. ← previous video.
M NME / NOT ME: identity ruling for the whole video (stands on its own, no grade needed).
0speed 1× → 1.5× → 2× → 3× → 4×.

A video that still has grey sections loops until you grade or skip it; a fully graded one moves on by itself. Grey sections are fine: the factory picks HERO first, avoids NEVER, and only uses ungraded sections when nothing better exists.

  • HERO This is the featured person, face showing, good shot. It joins the settled pool and is the first pick for every shot where the reference shows a person.
  • B-ROLL Fine as a background. Used only behind captions, where the reference shows no person. No face needed.
  • NEVER Never cast again, in any slot.
  • ME / NOT ME Identity for the whole video, by its number.

NO FACE means the face scan found nobody facing the camera. It can still be the featured person from behind or far away — watch before you grade. Mark all remaining NO-FACE as B-ROLL grades whole videos, and only ones with no grade on any section yet; a section grade always outranks a whole-video grade. If a tap shows red NOT saved, it did not land: tap it again.
