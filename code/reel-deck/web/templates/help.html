How to review

Watch it first — the whole system, 2½ minutes

Narrated: signing in, the board, watching a cut, commenting on a frame, answering the factory, giving a verdict, and adding a new reel. Everything below is the same thing in writing.

What this is

Reels get made by a machine. You are the eyes. You watch a reel next to the reference it studies, and you tell the machine yes, no, or here's what to change. That's the whole job.

{% if editor %}

Your role: editor

You watch reels and leave notes. Your notes and your "needs changes" go to the factory exactly like anyone's, and the factory acts on notes. Your approval is advice: a reel is only final when the owner approves it, so an "approve" from you tells the owner you think it is ready, it does not release anything.

You will not see Make, Now or the Nudge button. Those belong to the owner's login, and you do not need them to review.

{% endif %}

The board

Needs you — reels waiting for your verdict. Start here.
In the machine — the factory has your note. Usually it is building or fixing. If the card says QUEUED it has the note but is waiting on something (disk space, a usage limit) and nothing is building yet; the card says what it is waiting for. Nothing for you to do either way.
Done — approved or posted.
Parked — set aside on purpose.

The count at the top is live. If "Needs you" is 0, you're finished for now.

A red SENT · NOT PICKED UP YET on a card, or a red "orders not picked up" note at the top, means a note was sent more than 5 minutes ago and the factory has not picked it up. That is a machine problem, not yours. Tell whoever runs it.

Older reels at the bottom of the board is the archive: reels from earlier weeks. Some of them still say "New version ready" because nobody ever gave a verdict on them. They are not your queue and are not counted at the top. Leave them unless you were asked to look at one.

Reviewing a reel

  1. Tap a card in Needs you. It's one screen: the cut on the left, the comments thread on the right.
  2. Watch. V004 / REFERENCE pills switch between the cut and the original. Space plays and pauses, ← → step one frame.
  3. Something wrong? Pause on it, type in the one comment box, press Enter. The ⏱ chip pins the comment to that exact frame (it's on by default). Tap the chip off for a comment about the whole cut. 📎 attaches a screenshot.
  4. Decided? Set the status pill (top right of the player):
    • Approved — this cut is the one. The factory marks it done.
    • Needs changes — the factory redoes it from your comments. It needs a reason: your comments, or one line in the box.
    • In progress / Needs review — just for you, nothing is sent.

What happens after you send

Send / the status pill flashes SENT ✓. Your comment appears in the thread at once, with its timecode and a marker on the scrub bar. Its state:

○ waiting the factory hasn't picked it up yet (normal for a few minutes).
✓ received the factory has it and replied in the thread. ✓✓ means it finished that work.
! no answer the factory has been quiet too long. Tell whoever runs it.

Click any ⏱ timecode in the thread and the player jumps there.

When the factory asks you something

An amber card at the top of the thread is the factory asking you a question. There is only one place to write: the comment box under the video. While a question is open the box turns amber and says Answering the factory's question, and what you type goes back as your answer. Press Enter. Tap the ✕ on that amber line to leave a normal comment instead.

Answer these first — until you do, that row waits. The factory checks about every 3 minutes, so give it a couple of minutes before you think it was ignored.

Adding a new reel

Top of the page, Make. Paste the link of the reference reel you want to study/recreate (with permission), add a note if there is one ("recreate 1:1", "use the new footage"), press Send to the factory.

The factory registers it as a new row, studies the original, then builds a first cut. It shows up on the board under In the machine, and moves to Needs you when there is something to watch. First cuts take a while; you do not have to wait on the page.

The Nudge button

Top right, Nudge the factory. It tells the machine "carry on where you left off". Use it only if something has been sitting unanswered for a long time. It builds nothing by itself and can be pressed once every 10 minutes.

{% endif %}

If something looks wrong

A red message will tell you what to do. If a video won't play, the row will say the file is missing. If you're sent back to the sign-in page, your session expired — just sign in again; nothing you sent is lost. Anything else: tell whoever gave you your login.
